HOW THE KHATA STAYS CLEAR

Every change is visible before it becomes a record.

PRINCIPLE 01 / 03

Confirm before save: Parsed commands are previews, not transactions.

Speaking or typing a command never touches your financial balance directly. BolHisab prepares a structured confirmation showing the exact customer, the exact amount, and the calculated balance. The entry is recorded only when you physically tap Haan, save karein.

PRINCIPLE 02 / 03

Balances come from the ledger: Mathematical integrity in integer paise.

No language model or external API calculates your ledger balance. All math is performed locally using integer paise (₹1 = 100 paise) from immutable ledger entries. Payments cannot exceed outstanding dues, preventing negative balances or bookkeeping drift.

PRINCIPLE 03 / 03

Shopkeeper stays in control: Your data stays inside your device.

All records reside in your browser's native IndexedDB. BolHisab does not transmit your customer list, balances, or transaction history to any third-party cloud or database. You can export clean JSON or CSV backups or reset your ledger anytime.

// LOCAL PARSER VS ONLINE AI

BolHisab includes a built-in deterministic local command parser that recognizes everyday Hindi and Hinglish phrasing without requiring any API key or internet access. If an optional server-side AI key is configured, only the raw command text is sent for intent extraction; customer identities, contact numbers, and ledger history are never transmitted.

// REVERSIBILITY & AUDIT TRAIL

Mistakes happen during rush hours. BolHisab provides an instant Undo action right after confirmation, and allows explicit reversal entries for recent transactions. Entries maintain an auditable timeline instead of silently deleting historical bookkeeping records.
